This week we added less and made things more solid. Here's what changes for you, plainly.

**Verikora — reading a rental file**
- The report doesn't give a grade. It shows three levels of signal: what holds together, what draws attention, what doesn't match — and, each time, the line that proves it.
- The pieces of an applicant's file now have a place of their own, instead of sitting in an email.

**Krizaka — our public face**
- We put up a real contact page: a form, not an address thrown into the void.
- We named, publicly, the person responsible for your personal information. If you want to know who looks after your data, it's written down.

**Security**
- We fixed a few security points. It's the kind of work you don't see, but we do it every week.

We build slowly, in the open: the code is available to read, and we write here what's true, not what we'd like to promise. See you next week.
